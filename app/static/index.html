    Spatial searches apply a coordinate bounding-box rejection filter prior to computing trigonometric spherical geodesics, eliminating >92% of stores in O(1) time and sustaining sub-150ms p95 query latency.

    Multi-farmer concurrent holds execute with thread-safe atomic decrements. Held stock auto-restocks upon 4-hour timer expiration, preventing inventory lockouts.
